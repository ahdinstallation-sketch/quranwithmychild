Privacy policy — Quran With My Child

Last updated 7 October 2026.

The "Quran With My Child Uploader" is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.

It accesses only the channel owner's own YouTube account, to upload videos and change their visibility. It does not collect, store, sell or share data about anyone else. Credentials are kept privately by the owner and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
